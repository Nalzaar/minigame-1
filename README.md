# Minigame 1
## Devlog
Components are pretty much like the different parts of one's body, such as arms and legs. Together they form the body or in this case the GameObjects. The GameObjects are like the inhabitants of a world, with the world in this scenario being Scenes. They can also be viewed as different planes of existence or universes considering that there can also be multiple scenes within a single project.
